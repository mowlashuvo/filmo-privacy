Delete Your Filmo Account

Filmo respects your privacy and gives you the option to permanently delete your account and associated personal data.

1. How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion directly from the Filmo app:

  1. Open the Filmo application.
  2. Navigate to the Profile section.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion information and confirm your request.
  5. Complete any required identity verification.

Once the deletion process is successfully completed, your Firebase Authentication account and associated data will be deleted.

2. Request Deletion Without Access to the App

If you cannot access the Filmo application, you can contact our support team to request account deletion.

Please include the email address associated with your Filmo account so that we can verify your request.

Support email: Replace this text with your actual support email address.

3. Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account is successfully deleted, the following data associated with your account will be removed:

  • Your Filmo user profile information.
  • Your movie and TV show watchlist.
  • Reviews submitted by your account.
  • Ratings submitted by your account.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account.

4. Data Retention

Filmo does not intend to retain the personal account data listed above after successful account deletion.

Some information may remain in backups or be retained where required by applicable law or legitimate legal obligations. Any such data will be retained only for the required period.

5. Processing Time

Account deletion through the application is initiated after successful verification. Requests submitted through support will be reviewed and processed as soon as reasonably possible.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about account deletion or data privacy, please contact the Filmo support team using the support email address provided above.
