Delete Your Filmo Account

Filmo respects your privacy and gives you control over your personal information. You can request permanent deletion of your Filmo account and the personal data associated with it.

1. How to Delete Your Account in the Filmo App

If you can access the Filmo app, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Filmo application.
  2. Go to the Profile section.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the deletion information and confirm your request.
  5. Complete any required identity verification.

Once deletion is successfully completed, your Filmo account and associated data will be deleted as described below.

2. Request Account Deletion Without Using the App

You do not need to reinstall or access the Filmo app to request account deletion. You can submit a request directly by email.

Submit an account deletion request

Send an email to our support team with the subject Filmo Account Deletion Request.

Email Account Deletion Request

Support email: information.petguardian@gmail.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • The email address associated with your Filmo account, if applicable.
  • Your Filmo user ID, if available.
  • A clear statement that you want your Filmo account and associated data deleted.

For anonymous (guest) accounts that do not have an associated email address, provide your Filmo user ID if available. Our team may ask for reasonable additional information to verify ownership.

Never send your password or authentication credentials by email.

3. Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is successfully completed, Filmo will delete the following account-associated data:

  • Account information: Your Filmo profile, including your user ID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL, where stored.
  • Watchlist: Movies and TV shows saved to your personal watchlist.
  • Reviews: Reviews submitted through your account.
  • Ratings: Movie and TV show ratings submitted through your account.
  • Authentication information: Your associated Firebase Authentication account.

This deletion applies to data associated with your account in Filmo's systems. It does not delete information that other users may have independently copied or saved outside Filmo.

4. Data Retention

Filmo does not intend to retain the account information listed above after a successful deletion request.

If backup copies exist, they may remain in protected backup storage for up to [INSERT VERIFIED BACKUP RETENTION PERIOD] before being permanently overwritten or deleted through the applicable backup lifecycle.

Information may also be retained where necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud and abuse. Such information will be limited to what is necessary and retained only for the applicable required period.

5. Processing Time

We aim to complete verified account deletion requests within 30 calendar days of receiving the request. If additional verification is necessary or an exceptional circumstance delays processing, we will inform you.

6. Important Information

  • Deleting the Filmo app from your device does not delete your account.
  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Your watchlist, reviews, ratings, and profile information associated with the deleted account will no longer be available.
  • You may create a new account later, but previously deleted account data may not be recoverable.
